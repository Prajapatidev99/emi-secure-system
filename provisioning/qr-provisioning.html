📱 How QR Provisioning Works

No USB cable, no laptop needed! Just scan a QR code on the fresh phone.
1
Customer phone is fresh (new or after factory reset) — showing "Welcome" screen
2
Tap 6 times on any empty area of the Welcome screen → QR reader opens
3
Scan the QR code below → Phone auto-connects to Wi-Fi
4
Phone auto-downloads EMI Secure APK from your server
5
App is automatically set as Device Owner — all security enforced!
6
Add your shop Google account → Hardware FRP activated

⚠️ Important Notes

            

                QR provisioning only works on FRESH phones — either brand new (first boot) or right after factory reset. The Setup Wizard must be showing the "Welcome" / "Hi there" screen.
            

            

                Android 7.0+ required. QR code provisioning is available from Android 7.0 (Nougat) onwards. Most phones sold today support this.
            

            

                Tap location varies by brand:

                • Stock Android / Pixel: Tap 6x on "Welcome" text

                • Samsung: Tap 6x on "Let's go" or "Start" area

                • Xiaomi/Redmi: Tap 6x anywhere on the welcome screen

                • Realme/Oppo: Tap 6x on the empty area

                • Some phones: May need to connect to Wi-Fi first, then tap 6x
